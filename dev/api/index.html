CutPotentials.CutPotentials — Module
CutPotentials

Newton potentials of a smooth density screened by a piecewise-constant coefficient,

U(y) = ∫ G(y, x) a(x) ρ(x) dx,    G = 1/(4π|y - x|),   a = a_j on regions Ω_j, a0 outside,

with ∇U and ∇∇U, at arbitrary targets (inside or outside the regions, near or on their surfaces). Every spectral or point-charge computation acts on the smooth, unscreened ρ (SmoothField: TKM near the samples, SCTL FMM elsewhere); the jumps of aρ become surface integrals of the smooth fields φ0 = N[ρ], ψ = ∇φ0, H = ∇ψ over the interfaces (Interface, SurfaceQuadrature: SCTL Gauss-Legendre elements graded toward the density, FMM plus SCTL near corrections, CutPlan).

Workflow:

src   = VolumeSource(values, origin, basis)                  # samples on a lattice
field = SmoothField(src; tol = 1e-10)
itf   = vcat(box_interfaces(boxes, a, a0), [sphere_interface(c, R, a_in - a_out)])
sq    = SurfaceQuadrature(itf; support = density_support(src), L0 = σ)
plan  = CutPlan(sq, targets; a0, hess = true, side = side_normals)
r     = evaluate(plan, field)                                 # r.pot, r.grad, r.hess, r.coef

Conventions: interface normals point from side A to side B and δa = a_A - a_B; Hessian rows are ∂xx, ∂yy, ∂zz, ∂xy, ∂xz, ∂yz; on-surface Hessians are one-sided limits on the side the target's side vector points to.

CutPotentials.CutPlan — Type
CutPlan(sq::SurfaceQuadrature, targets; a0, pot = true, grad = true, hess = false, side = nothing,
        hess_offset = 1e-5 * sq.L0, tol = 1e-12, fmm_digits = nothing)

Everything that depends on the geometry and the targets only, for evaluating U = N[a ρ] (and derivatives) at targets (3 × Nt) for any density by evaluate: SCTL's near corrections (accurate minus node rule, at accuracy tol) of the double layer (Laplace3D_DxU), the single layer (Laplace3D_FxU) and, for hess, the single-layer gradient (Laplace3D_FxdU) from the nodes of sq to the targets, and SCTL ParticleFMM trees for their node rules (fmm_digits, default -log10(tol) + 2, at most 15). a0 is the background coefficient.

On-surface targets. Potential and gradient need no flag: the formulas used are continuous across the interfaces (see evaluate), so a target on a surface, or within rounding of it, gets the (unique) value. The Hessian jumps across an interface; for a target on a surface give its column of side (3 × Nt, zero columns for the other targets): a vector pointing into the side whose limit is wanted. That Hessian is extrapolated from y + kδν̂ (k = 1..4; δ = hess_offset), H ≈ 4H(δ) - 6H(2δ) + 4H(3δ) - H(4δ), with relative error about (δ/ℓ)⁴, ℓ the distance to the nearest edge or corner of the interfaces (where H is singular) or the length scale of the density. Keep δ well above the geometric error of curved elements (the shifted points must be on the wanted side of the discretized surface). Unflagged targets are evaluated where they are; a target exactly in the plane of a flat face gets the two-sided average of the Hessian.

CutPotentials.Interface — Type
Interface(patches, δa; name = "")

A piece of the interfaces between regions of constant coefficient, with the jump δa = a_A - a_B across it. patches is a vector of functions X(u, v) ((u, v) ∈ [0, 1]², returning a point as 3 numbers), smooth on the closed parameter square, whose normal ∂X/∂u × ∂X/∂v points from side A to side B. Each patch is one or more SCTL Gauss-Legendre elements after refinement (SurfaceQuadrature).

The interfaces given to SurfaceQuadrature must together form the boundaries of the regions: for each region Ωj (coefficient aj, background a0 outside all of them), its closed boundary ∂Ωj, oriented outward, is covered by pieces whose δa (with orientation) adds up to the region-by-region sum Σ_j (a_j - a_0) [∂Ω_j]. A face shared by two regions is given once, with δa = a_A - a_B; faces with δa = 0 can be dropped. Constructors: box_interfaces, sphere_interface, ellipsoid_interface.

CutPotentials.SmoothField — Type
SmoothField(src::VolumeSource; tol = 1e-10, c_pad = 5.0, kmax = nothing, window_sigma = 1.5,
            window_tol = tol * 1e-4, far_digits = nothing, nthreads = min(16, Threads.nthreads()))

The free-space Newton potential φ0 = N[ρ] (kernel 1/(4π|y - x|)) of the full, unscreened density of src, with its gradient ψ and Hessian H, ready for evaluation at any points (field_values).

  • Near the samples (inside the source box padded by c_pad · ‖basis‖₂): the truncated-kernel method. A type-1 NUFFT (FINUFFT, accuracy tol) of the charges, times the truncated Laplace kernel 2 (sin(Lk/2)/k)² for |k| ≤ kmax (default π/hmin), on one real scalar fine grid (oversampling `windowsigma). Potential, gradient and Hessian are gathered from that grid with the window w, w′ and w″ of an analytic ES window tapered by (1 - z²)³ (window_tol` sets its width).
  • Elsewhere: point charges weights .* density by SCTL's FMM (Laplace3D_FxU, _FxdU, _Fxd2U) at far_digits digits (default -log10(tol) + 5, at most 15).

Accuracy is limited by tol and by the sampling of ρ (spectrally accurate for a smooth, well-resolved density).

CutPotentials.SurfaceQuadrature — Type
SurfaceQuadrature(interfaces; support, L0, κ = 0.5, order = 12, max_size = Inf, max_depth = 24)

The source discretization of the surface integrals: every patch of interfaces (a vector of Interface) is split (quadtree in its parameter square) until each element's size is at most min(max_size, κ (d + L0)), d its distance to the box support = (lo, hi) of the density (e.g. density_support). The integrands vary on the density's scale near it and like the field's distance farther away; L0 (a length, e.g. the density's width) sets the finest size κ L0. All elements form one SCTL QuadElemList of order² Gauss-Legendre nodes (order in 4, 8, 12, 16, 20). Stores the nodes X, unit normals normal (from A to B), weights w and the jump δa per node.

The discretization depends on the geometry and the support only: reuse it for every density with that support.

CutPotentials.VolumeSource — Type
VolumeSource(positions, weights, density, basis)
VolumeSource(values::AbstractArray{<:Real,3}, origin, basis)
VolumeSource(f::Function, origin, basis, dims)

Samples density of a smooth density ρ at the points positions (3 × N), with quadrature weights (cell volumes), on a lattice with primitive step vectors basis (a 3×3 matrix whose column j is the step along lattice direction j, or a tuple of three 3-vectors). The point-charge (and spectral) representation of ρ is Σ_s weights[s] density[s] δ(x - positions[s]).

The lattice forms build the samples x_ijk = origin + basis * (i-1, j-1, k-1) with weights |det basis|, from an array of values or from a function f(x) sampled on dims = (n1, n2, n3) points.

The basis is used for the near/far geometry of the TKM field (SmoothField): the characteristic spacing h = ‖basis‖₂, the source box (the bounding box of the samples inflated by half a cell) and the spectral cutoff kmax = π / h_min with h_min the shortest lattice vector.

CutPotentials.box_interfaces — Method
box_interfaces(boxes, a, a0; rtol = 1e-12) -> Vector{Interface}

The interfaces of a union of axis-aligned boxes with disjoint interiors: box j is (lo, hi) (two 3-tuples) with coefficient a[j], the background has a0. Face coordinates of all boxes are merged within rtol (relative), so neighbouring boxes share faces exactly; the faces are cut into the cells of the grid of all face coordinates, and each cell where the coefficient jumps becomes one rectangle with normal +e_k (from the x[k] < c side A to the x[k] > c side B) and δa = a_A - a_B. Rectangles with equal coefficients on both sides are dropped.

CutPotentials.cut_potential — Method
cut_potential(src, interfaces, targets; a0, tol = 1e-10, support = density_support(src), L0, κ = 0.5,
              order = 12, pot = true, grad = true, hess = false, side = nothing, quad_tol = 1e-12, kw...)

One-shot convenience: SmoothField of src (accuracy tol), SurfaceQuadrature of interfaces graded toward support (default L0: a quarter of the smallest side of the support box, at least 2‖basis‖), a CutPlan at targets and evaluate. For many densities or target batches build the pieces once.

CutPotentials.ellipsoid_interface — Method
ellipsoid_interface(center, semiaxes, δa; R = I, nsub = 2, name = "ellipsoid") -> Interface

The ellipsoid center + R * (semiaxes .* s), s on the unit sphere, as a cubed sphere: 6 × nsub² patches, each the equiangular map of a square of a cube face, normalized onto the sphere, then scaled and rotated (R a 3×3 rotation). Normals point outward: side A is the inside, δa = a_inside - a_outside.

CutPotentials.evaluate — Method
evaluate(plan::CutPlan, field::SmoothField) -> (; pot, grad, hess, coef, timings)

U(y) = ∫ G(y, x) a(x) ρ(x) dx (G = 1/(4π|y - x|)) at the plan's targets, with grad = ∇U (3 × Nt) and hess = ∇∇U (6 × Nt, rows ∂xx, ∂yy, ∂zz, ∂xy, ∂xz, ∂yz), those not requested nothing. coef is the coefficient the formulas see at each target, a0 - W(y) with W = Σ_m δa_m D_m[1]: a(y) off the interfaces, (aA + aB)/2 on a smooth point of an interface, Σj αj a_j (solid-angle fractions) at edges and corners.

With φ0, ψ = ∇φ0 and H = ∇ψ from field (the full, smooth density), the merged identities (Green's third identity per region, the two copies of each shared face combined, W from the same quadrature) are

U   = (a0 - W) φ0 + Σ_m δa_m (D_m[φ0] - S_m[ψ·n])
∇U  = (a0 - W) ψ  + Σ_m δa_m (D_m[ψ]  - S_m[H n - tr(H) n])
∇∂_iU = (a0 - W) H e_i + Σ_m δa_m (∇×S_m[n × H e_i] - ∇S_m[(H n)_i - tr(H) n_i])

all from nodal values of φ0, ψ, H on the surface elements (no differentiation on panels). The first two equal a0 φ0 + Σ δa D[φ0 - φ0(y)] - ..., whose double layers have densities vanishing at y: continuous through the surfaces, rounding-safe. The Hessian (the curl term is ∇D[ψ_i] by Maue's identity) is symmetrized.

CutPotentials.field_values — Method
field_values(f::SmoothField, Y; order = 2) -> (; pot, grad, hess)

φ0 (vector), ψ = ∇φ0 (3 × n) and, for order = 2, H = ∇∇φ0 (6 × n, rows ∂xx, ∂yy, ∂zz, ∂xy, ∂xz, ∂yz) at the points Y (3 × n). order = 1 skips the Hessian (hess = nothing).
